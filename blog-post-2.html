5 Link Building Strategies That Still Work in 2026

The link building tactics that still move rankings in 2026: digital PR, niche edits, topical relevance, linkable assets and smarter content upgrades.

Nadeem Mayo·April 24, 2026

Link building has changed a lot, but links from relevant, trusted websites are still one of the strongest ranking signals. These are the five approaches we rely on most for clients today.

1. Digital PR and news mentions

Getting your brand featured in news and industry publications builds authority and trust at the same time. Data stories, expert commentary and timely reactions to industry news are the most reliable ways to earn these mentions.

2. Niche edits on established pages

A niche edit adds a relevant link to an existing article that is already indexed and receiving traffic. Because the page already has history and trust, the link can start contributing faster than one on a brand-new post.

3. Relevance over raw numbers

A few links from sites that share your audience usually beat many links from unrelated sites with higher metrics. We always check topical fit and organic traffic before domain rating.

4. Linkable assets

Original research, free tools, templates and clear visual guides naturally attract links over time. One good asset can support an entire outreach campaign.

5. Improve on what already earns links

Find content in your niche that has attracted lots of links, create a version that is genuinely more useful and up to date, and reach out to the sites linking to the older version.

Quality, relevance and pace matter more than volume. Build links the way a growing brand would naturally earn them.
