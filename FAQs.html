FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most

Everything about how we work, our SEO and link building, websites, ad management and payments.

Getting started

Who do you work with?

We work with small and medium businesses, e-commerce brands, SaaS companies and marketing agencies, mainly in the United States, United Kingdom and Canada.

How do I get started?

Fill in the contact form or message us on WhatsApp. We will review your website, ask a few questions and send a proposal, usually within 24 hours.

Do you offer a free audit?

Yes. We offer a free initial review covering your website, rankings and the biggest opportunities we can see.

SEO & link building

Are your backlinks safe?

Yes. We only place links on real websites with organic traffic and relevant content. We don't use private blog networks or link farms.

How long until guest posts go live?

Most guest posts go live within 5–10 working days after content approval. Premium news sites may take longer.

What happens if a link is removed?

If a link is removed within the guarantee period, we replace it with a link of similar quality at no extra cost.

Do you write the content for guest posts?

Yes. Content writing is included with guest posts unless you prefer to supply your own article.

How long does SEO take to work?

Early improvements often appear within 6–12 weeks. Stronger results typically build over 4–6 months, depending on competition.

Web development

What platforms do you build on?

WordPress, Shopify, Webflow and fully custom code, depending on what suits your business best.

How long does a website take?

Most business websites take 2–4 weeks. Online stores and custom builds usually take 4–8 weeks.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Once the project is paid in full, you own the design, code and content.

Paid ads

Which ad platforms do you manage?

Google Ads (Search, Performance Max, Shopping and YouTube), Meta Ads across Facebook and Instagram, and TikTok Ads.

Is ad spend included in your fee?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to the platform from your own account. Our fee covers strategy, setup and management.

Do I keep ownership of my ad accounts?

Always. Campaigns run in your accounts and you keep full access to all data.

Payments & agencies

What payment methods do you accept?

PayPal, Payoneer, Wise and major credit and debit cards.

Do you offer white-label services?

Yes. We work as a silent partner for agencies, with unbranded reports and no direct client contact unless you request it.

Do you require long contracts?

No. Monthly services are rolling, and projects are quoted individually.

Still have a question?

Message us and we will answer within a few hours.
