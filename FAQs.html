General SEO Questions

What is off-page SEO? +
Off-page SEO includes all activities done outside your website to improve rankings, authority, and visibility. This includes guest posting, backlinks, niche edits, brand mentions, digital PR, and link building.
Why is off-page SEO important? +
Off-page SEO helps search engines understand your website authority and trust. Quality backlinks from relevant websites can improve rankings, organic traffic, and brand credibility.
What is the difference between on-page SEO and off-page SEO? +
On-page SEO focuses on content, meta tags, headings, speed, and internal structure. Off-page SEO focuses on backlinks, guest posts, mentions, and external authority signals.
Can backlinks improve Google rankings? +
Yes, quality backlinks can support better rankings when they come from relevant, trusted, indexed, and real-traffic websites.
