FAQ

Google Ads: common questions

What budget do I need?

It depends on your market. Many local businesses start at $1,000–$3,000 per month in ad spend. We recommend a budget during the audit.

Is ad spend included in your fee?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Google from your own account. Our fee covers management.

Do I keep my account?

Yes. Campaigns run in your account and you keep full ownership and access.
